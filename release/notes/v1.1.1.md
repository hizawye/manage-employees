### What's new

- Plan weekly and overnight shifts, reuse schedule templates, assign open shifts and review leave requests in the new Work tab.
- Track clock-ins and breaks, review payroll periods and export timesheets, payroll reports and schedules.
- Open **Profile → App updates** to check for newer Android releases. The app also checks periodically in the background when you open it.
